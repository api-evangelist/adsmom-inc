# Adsmom

> AI-powered ad intelligence platform. Adsmom indexes 200M+ ads from Meta, TikTok and Google, decodes them with AI, and gives brands, agencies and research teams a clear read on what competitors are doing — and what's actually working.

## Product

- [Explore Ads](https://adsmom.com/product/explore): Search 200M+ competitor ads across Meta, TikTok and Google in one library. Filter by brand, platform, format and trend signals.
- [Ad Insights](https://adsmom.com/product/insights): Decode competitor strategy — spend signals, creative angles, performance patterns — without manual digging.
- [AI Intelligence (Sense)](https://adsmom.com/product/intelligence): Ask plain-English strategic questions about any tracked brand. Sense answers using the full Adsmom ad corpus.
- [Real-Time Alerts](https://adsmom.com/product/alerts): Get notified the moment a tracked competitor launches a new campaign, changes creative or shifts spend.
- [Reporting](https://adsmom.com/product/reporting): Generate branded competitive intel reports for stakeholders — schedule, share and export in a click.
- [API](https://adsmom.com/product/api): Put the Adsmom ad index inside your own tools. Connect an AI assistant over MCP, or call the REST API from your code.

## Solutions

- [For Brands](https://adsmom.com/solutions/brands): Marketing teams watching their category, learning what creative actually performs, and reacting before the market shifts.
- [For Agencies](https://adsmom.com/solutions/agencies): Walk into pitches with category-wide competitive intel, build sharper creative briefs, prove ROI with hard numbers.
- [For Research](https://adsmom.com/solutions/research): Use Adsmom as a queryable corpus of 200M+ ads for trend analysis, panel studies and market sizing.
